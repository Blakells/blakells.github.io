## The game

You take a lantern down five floors of the under-garden. The stairs are somewhere in the dark, the oil is
always running low, and the local grumbles are having a day. Walking into a grumble soothes it: nothing in
this dungeon needs killing, and there is no game over.

## How it's built

- Plain JavaScript on Canvas 2D, with no build step and no dependencies. The art is procedural geometry,
  with no art assets.
- Every floor is generated from a seed, so `?seed=53` replays the same dungeon.
- The light radius shrinks as the lantern's oil burns down; oil flasks and campfires refill it.
- A minimap in the corner.
- On an iPhone it gets a touch pad and two action buttons, and it installs to the Home Screen as an app.
- A small test for the world generator, `test-world.js`.
