## The game

You wash ashore on a small island with a backpack, a few tools and a tent. From there it is yours to
shape: chop wood and build a house block by block, step under the roof to decorate inside, comb the beach
for shells each morning, fish off the pier, plant a garden, and get to know the three neighbours and the
otter who runs the trading post. There is no way to lose and nothing to hurry for.

- 40 kinds of building block, and about 70 pieces of furniture and decoration.
- 13 fish across the sea and the lagoon, some only at night, plus butterflies and glowbugs to catch.
- Cats, dogs, bunnies, crabs, ducklings and foxes to befriend and adopt.
- Day and night, rain and rainbows, shooting stars to wish on, and a rowboat out to the lighthouse.

## How it's built

- Plain JavaScript with Three.js, with no build step: 23 script files, loaded in order.
- The island is a voxel grid generated from a seed. A save stores the seed and the cells that differ from
  it, so it stays small enough for the browser's local storage.
- Only the things you build are drawn as blocks. The ground is drawn as one sloped surface from averaged
  corner heights, trees get round canopies, and shells, flowers and small decorations are rounded shapes.
- No art or audio files. Every texture is painted onto a canvas when the page loads, and the sound is
  synthesised with the Web Audio API.
- On a touch screen it switches to touch controls: a walking stick under the left thumb, tap to use or
  place, hold to dig, and buttons for the rest. It ships a web app manifest and icon for a phone's Home
  Screen.
